Hitchwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Hitchwise collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no network request. Your rotation stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Hitchwise has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks and contains no networking code at all.

Where your data lives

  • Your rotation is stored as a file on your device, inside the app's own container and a shared App Group container that lets the widgets read it.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Hitchwise makes no network connections, so your schedule cannot leave your device through this app.
  • If you use iCloud device backup, your Hitchwise file is included in that backup, encrypted and controlled by Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

Why this matters for a rotation schedule

Your schedule says exactly when you are away from home and when the house is empty. That is not information we want to hold, so we built the app so that we cannot hold it.

Sharing your schedule

Hitchwise can create a standard calendar file (.ics) of your rotation and hand it to iOS's share sheet. That file is generated on your device. Where it goes next is entirely your choice — Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or saving it to Files. Once you hand it to another app, that app's own privacy policy applies. Hitchwise never sends it anywhere on its own, and has no way to.

Your control

  • Erase — Settings lets you clear all data at any time. Deleting the app also removes everything.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us.

Children

Hitchwise is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Should a future version of Hitchwise ever add a feature that transmits data, that will be stated plainly here and in the app before it ships.
