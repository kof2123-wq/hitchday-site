Hitchday — Support

A calendar for people who work rotational shifts.

Email kof2123@gmail.com. Tell me your rotation and what went wrong — that is usually enough to find it.

Setting your rotation

Pick your pattern (14/14, 7/7, 8/6, 21/21, or your own), then tap the day your current hitch started. That one date sets the whole schedule. If the app is showing the wrong day, it is almost always that start date — open Settings and tap Hitch started to correct it.

Covering a shift, or getting sent home early

Open the full calendar and tap the day. Change just that day. Your rotation keeps running underneath — nothing after it moves. This is the whole reason the app stores your pattern and your one-off changes separately.

Travel days

If you count a travel day at either end of your hitch, set it in Settings. Travel days appear in their own color, and the countdown treats them as "not home yet."

Sending your schedule to your family

Tap the share button on the main screen, or Send my schedule in Settings. It creates a calendar file covering the next year. Send it however you like — the person receiving it opens it and their calendar app adds your hitches. They do not need this app.

If you change your rotation later, send a fresh copy. The old file does not update on its own.

Starting a new job

Settings → Started a new job. Your old schedule is kept, so you can still look up where you were last July. Do not edit the old one — record a new one.

Widgets

Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap the + or Customize, and find Hitchday. If the widget looks stale, open the app once — widgets refresh from the app's stored schedule.

No signal on location

That is fine. Hitchday makes no network requests. Everything is computed on your phone from the pattern you set.

Does it handle daylight saving?

Yes. The rotation is counted in calendar days, not hours, and every supported pattern is verified day by day across years of dates before each release — including both DST switches and leap days.
